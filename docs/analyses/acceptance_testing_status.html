The Gating Standard for Alternative Gravity Theories

In standard peer review at journals such as Physical Review D, Classical and Quantum Gravity, and Astrophysical Journal, alternative gravitational frameworks are subjected to an immediate 5-tier verification hierarchy. Theoretical physics referees prioritize fundamental mathematical and high-acceleration constraints before reviewing large-scale cosmological data:

  • Rejection of Subjective Qualitative Labels: Qualitative labels like "Good", "Excellent", or "Fail" are discounted. Peer review demands quantitative metrics: reduced chi-square (χ²/dof), RMS scatter, Bayesian information criteria (ΔBIC), and parameter penalty counts (k).
  • Mandatory Gating Gaps: Frameworks that fail PPN solar system bounds (|γ - 1| ≤ 2.3 × 10⁻⁵), gravitational wave speed (|c_GW - c|/c < 10⁻¹⁵ from GW170817), or exhibit Ostrogradsky ghost instabilities are ruled out before evaluating rotation curves or cosmological fits.
  • Isolation of Distinct Cosmological Epochs: Cosmological observables cannot be conflated into broad categories. The primordial acoustic sound horizon (r_d), primary CMB angular spectrum (TT, TE, EE), Baryon Acoustic Oscillations (BAO), cosmic shear (S_8), and Type Ia Supernovae represent distinct physical regimes and must be verified independently.
