1. Executive Summary & Space Density Ratio Corrections

This investigation combines ALL observational standard candle datasets across 4 orders of magnitude in redshift (\(0.001 \le z \le 14.32\)) and compares Raw, Calibrated, and CR Space-Field Corrected samples:

  • 1,701 Pantheon+ Supernovae Ia (\(0.0012 \le z \le 2.26\))
  • Quasars: Raw, Standard Calibrated (\(+2.85\text{ mag}\)), and CR Space-Field Corrected (\(\delta \mu = -0.298 \log_{10}(1+z)\text{ mag}\)).
  • Gamma-Ray Bursts (GRBs): Raw Literature Baseline and CR Space-Field Corrected (\(\delta \mu = -0.149 \log_{10}(1+z)\text{ mag}\) from Amati relation spectral peak energy scaling).
  • JWST Early Massive Galaxies (GN-z11 at \(z=10.60\) & JADES-GS-z14-0 at \(z=14.32\))
Observational Data Reliability Note: Quasar standard candle data (\(L_{\text{X}} - L_{\text{UV}}\) relation) is included for historical completeness, but is considered significantly less reliable by the technical community compared to SNe Ia, GRBs, and JWST early galaxies due to uncorrected Malmquist selection bias at \(z > 2\).
