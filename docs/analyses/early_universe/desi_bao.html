1. Executive Summary & Observational Dataset

In April 2024, the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI Collaboration, Adame et al. 2024) published the largest and most precise Baryon Acoustic Oscillation (BAO) measurements to date, utilizing over 6 million galaxies and quasars spanning the redshift range \(z = 0.295\) to \(z = 2.330\).

DESI reported a provocative \(2.5\sigma - 3.9\sigma\) tension with the standard flat \(\Lambda\text{CDM}\) model when combined with CMB and SNe Ia, sparking intense debate over whether dark energy evolves over cosmic time (\(w_0 > -1, w_a < 0\)). In Chronodynamic Relativity (CR), cosmic expansion is strictly linear (\(R(t) \propto t\)), and the apparent deviation in expansion distance emerges naturally from environmental metric clock dilation \(\eta(z) = (1+z)^{n/2}\) without dynamical dark energy fields.

This forces standard parametrizations into dynamical quintessence (\(w_0 w_a\text{CDM}\)).
