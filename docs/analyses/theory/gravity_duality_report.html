$$\frac{d^2 x^i}{dt^2} = \left(\frac{dt}{d\lambda}\right)^{-2} \left[ \frac{d^2 x^i}{d\lambda^2} - \frac{dx^i}{d\lambda} \left(\frac{dt}{d\lambda}\right)^{-1} \frac{d^2 t}{d\lambda^2} \right]$$

$$\frac{d^2 x^i}{dt^2} = \left(\frac{dt}{d\lambda}\right)^{-2} \frac{d^2 x^i}{d\lambda^2} + v^i \left( \vec{\nabla}\ln A \cdot \vec{v} \right)$$

Evaluating the spatial Christoffel term $\tilde{\Gamma}^i_{jk} v^j v^k$:

$$\tilde{\Gamma}^i_{jk} v^j v^k = \frac{1}{2} \left( v^i \left( \vec{v} \cdot \vec{\nabla}\ln A \right) + v^i \left( \vec{v} \cdot \vec{\nabla}\ln A \right) - v^2 \partial_i \ln A \right) = v^i \left( \vec{v} \cdot \vec{\nabla}\ln A \right) - \frac{1}{2} v^2 \partial_i \ln A$$

$$\frac{d^2 x^i}{dt^2} = -\left( \frac{c^2}{2} \partial_i \ln A \right) - \left( v^i \left( \vec{v} \cdot \vec{\nabla}\ln A \right) - \frac{1}{2} v^2 \partial_i \ln A \right) + v^i \left( \vec{v} \cdot \vec{\nabla}\ln A \right)$$

Notice that the cross-terms $v^i \left( \vec{v} \cdot \vec{\nabla}\ln A \right)$ cancel out completely! This leaves the exact coordinate acceleration:
