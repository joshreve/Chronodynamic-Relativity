1. Executive Summary & Foundational Physics

In standard Special Relativity, the Lorentz factor γ = (1 − v2/c2)−1/2 is an instantaneous, kinematic invariant of empty Minkowski geometry. As v → c, γ diverges to infinity, requiring infinite mechanical work to accelerate any finite mass.

In Chronodynamic Relativity (CR), spacetime geometry is mediated by the physical space-density field ρs(x, t), and time lapse is governed by the metric line element:

ds2 = −c2 ηdyn2(t) dt2 + dx2 = −c2 dτ2,   where ηdyn(t) ≡ dτ/dt

Under rapid acceleration, the body can out-accelerate the field effect. The effective Lorentz factor γdyn ≡ 1/ηdyn lags behind the kinematic velocity, permitting acceleration with near-Newtonian inertia and eliminating the infinite energy divergence.

In standard Special Relativity, the work integral diverges as v → c because the coordinate acceleration dv/dt ∝ (1 − v2/c2)3/2 drops to zero while momentum diverges.

  • When proper time ceases to advance, internal clocks stop, and no further proper work can be absorbed. The divergence is purely temporal, not an unphysical energy infinity.
